MIG-ESP® Interior Anti-Microbial

Interior — Residential & Commercial

MIG-ESP® Interior

The standard interior coating for residential and commercial spaces. MIG-ESP® Interior dramatically improves thermal comfort by reducing heat transfer through walls and ceilings by approximately 20% per coated surface.

Breathable formula regulates moisture naturally — no trapped dampness, no "cold wall" feeling in winter, no overheating in summer. Mineral-based with ultra-low VOC content under 1g/L.
