Featured Project

German Lutheran Trinity Church, East Melbourne

📍 East Melbourne, VIC — Interior Anti-Microbial

Heritage Church — Mould Eliminated. Heritage Preserved.

This heritage-listed church suffered from persistent moisture, flaking paint and a musty smell caused by moisture in the walls. MIG-ESP® Interior Anti-Microbial was applied throughout the nave and interior spaces — eliminating mould at the source and restoring the church's historic beauty.

"In October 2020 we renovated the interior of our heritage listed church as paint was flaking due to moisture in the walls. Walls were cold and there was a musty smell. Using MIG-ESP Interior the walls are now dry and warm. The musty smell is gone. The room climate is noticeably improved. The colour was matched to a T — its brilliance enhances the charm and dignity of our heritage church significantly."
— Helmut G., Building Committee, German Lutheran Trinity Church
Product: MIG-ESP® Interior Anti-Microbial Result: Mould-free. Dry. Warm. Heritage colours preserved perfectly
