Why MIG-ESP®

Standard Paint Fails. MIG-ESP® Doesn't.

Australian buildings face a brutal combination of heat, moisture and mould. Most coatings mask the problem. MIG-ESP® solves it.

❌ The Problem

  • Australian summers trap dangerous heat inside walls and roofs
  • Standard paint traps moisture — mould grows within months
  • Conventional insulation is expensive and difficult to retrofit
  • Flammable materials increase fire risk in commercial buildings
  • Chemical-laden paints harm indoor air quality
  • Re-coating every 2–3 years = ongoing cost and disruption

✅ The MIG-ESP® Solution

  • Reduces roof surface temperature by up to 25°C in direct sun
  • Keeps surfaces dry — mould physically cannot grow
  • Improves R-Value by 20% per coated surface — energy savings from day one
  • Non-flammable, tested to EN13501 and DIN4201
  • VOC content under 1g/L — safe for hospitals, schools, aged care
  • 8+ years proven mould-free — Bakery Lange, Germany (verified in writing)
