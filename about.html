Who We Are

MIG Pacific is the Australian distributor of the MIG-ESP® Active Coating System — an innovative, breathable, heat-reflective and anti-microbial wall and roof coating originally developed in Germany.

The product addresses what matters most in modern buildings: thermal comfort, energy savings, and the elimination of mould and germs — all while being easy to apply and cost-effective to maintain.

Today, MIG-ESP® has been installed across Australia, Germany, Spain, Israel, China and the UAE. From heritage churches in Melbourne to Siemens industrial facilities in Shanghai — the product performs wherever it's applied.
